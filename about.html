Our Mountain Heritage

From the Hills of Dhading to Direct Himalayan Expeditions

Namaste Hiking Trek was founded on a simple conviction born from the high trails: Himalayan adventure is at its finest when organized directly by the mountain professionals who actually guide the route.

Born in Dhading district, founder Lal Gurung began carrying packs and guiding international trekkers through the Everest, Annapurna, and Manaslu regions over 20 years ago. Having summited Mera Peak (6,476m) and led hundreds of high-pass expeditions, Lal witnessed how overseas brokers took massive commission markups while local village guides and porters were underpaid and ill-equipped.

In 2016, Namaste Hiking Trek was legally established in Kathmandu as a direct, licensed local operator. By eliminating overseas broker markups, we ensure fair living wages, quality winter gear, and comprehensive insurance for our mountain crews, while offering international adventurers personalized service, transparent pricing, and uncompromising high-altitude safety.

Uncompromising Field Protocols

Mountain Safety & Ethical Porter Welfare

Mountains are inherently unpredictable. We do not make reckless promises of "zero risk"—instead, we uphold rigorous, reviewable safety standards and humane treatment for our crews.

High-Altitude Safety Standards

  • Twice-Daily Health Logs: Lead guides record pulse oximeter readings (SpO2 oxygen saturation and pulse rate) every morning and evening.
  • Standard First Aid Kits: Carried on all departures, stocked with high-altitude pharmaceuticals, wound dressings, and rehydration salts.
  • High-Pass Oxygen: Supplementary emergency oxygen cylinders and masks are carried on technical high-pass routes (Three Passes, Dhaulagiri, Mera Peak).
  • Clear Evacuation Protocol: While travelers must carry personal travel insurance covering helicopter evacuation up to 6,000m, Namaste Hiking Trek handles immediate 24/7 flight coordination without requiring upfront cash on the trail.

Ethical Staff Welfare Standards

  • Strict 20–25 kg Load Limit: We enforce TAAN guidelines. Porters carry loads for maximum 2 trekkers, ensuring safe and humane trail conditions.
  • Company-Issued Mountain Apparel: Every porter receives windproof/waterproof jackets, insulated fleece, thermal gloves, boots, and UV-rated sunglasses.
  • Full Medical & Life Insurance: All field staff, guides, and porters are insured for emergency medical care and helicopter evacuation throughout the trek.
  • Fair Living Wages: Daily wages exceed national industry minimums, ensuring local families benefit directly from tourism.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions travelers ask about our legal credentials, trip planning, and emergency safety.

Is Namaste Hiking Trek a legally registered company in Nepal?

Yes, Namaste Hiking Trek Pvt. Ltd. is a fully authorized and tax-compliant adventure operator in Nepal. We hold Department of Tourism Operating Authorization, Inland Revenue Department PAN/VAT Registration #60124859, Kathmandu Metropolitan City business registration, and active memberships with TAAN (Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal) and NMA (Nepal Mountaineering Association). Official physical certificates are displayed at our Kathmandu headquarters.

What happens if I get Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) or injured?

All our lead guides are certified in Wilderness First Aid and monitor blood oxygen (SpO2) saturation daily. If severe symptoms occur, the lead guide immediately initiates descent. In urgent emergencies requiring medical evacuation, our Kathmandu operations office coordinates immediate helicopter rescue directly with your insurance provider.

How does your deposit and payment policy work?

We require a standard reservation deposit to lock in your guide, permits, and teahouse rooms. The remaining balance is payable upon arrival in Kathmandu via credit card, bank transfer, or cash. If your trip is disrupted by illness or cancelled flights, your deposit remains 100% valid for life or transferable to another traveler.

What are your porter welfare rules and load limits?

We strictly enforce a maximum load limit of 20–25 kg per porter, with one porter carrying gear for a maximum of two trekkers. Every porter receives company-issued cold-weather gear, medical and life insurance, and living wages well above baseline minimums.
