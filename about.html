Our Story & Heritage

Born in the Valleys of Solukhumbu

Founded over 15 years ago by veteran Sherpa mountaineers, Namaste Hiking Trek was established to bridge authentic mountain hospitality with uncompromising Western safety standards. Raised in the shadow of Mount Everest, our founders began as porters and junior guides before creating a direct, local agency that eliminates third-party commission markups.

Today, we operate comprehensive trekking, peak climbing, helicopter charters, and luxury tours across Nepal, Bhutan, and Tibet. Every dollar you spend directly empowers local high-altitude Sherpa communities, lodge owners, and porter families.
