About Namaste Hiking Trek

Licensed local agency committed to sustainable Himalayan trekking, ethical porter treatment, and high-altitude safety.

Founded by Himalayan Guides

Your Gateway to Authentic Nepal Treks

Namaste Hiking Trek was founded by veteran Sherpa guides who grew up in the mountain valleys of Khumbu and Solukhumbu. Having guided over 10,000 international trekkers across Everest, Annapurna, Manaslu, and Langtang, our agency combines deep indigenous knowledge with international safety protocols.

We operate directly without third-party middlemen—meaning your payments directly support local mountain communities, lodge owners, and porter families.
