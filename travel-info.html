Essential Nepal Trekking Guide

Everything you need to know before stepping foot in Nepal: Visa processes, high-altitude health protocols, travel insurance policies, and gear checklist.

🛂

Nepal Tourist Visa on Arrival

Most nationalities can easily obtain a Tourist Visa on Arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM) in Kathmandu.

  • 💵 15 Days Visa: USD $30
  • 💵 30 Days Visa: USD $50
  • 💵 90 Days Visa: USD $125
  • 📋 Passport must have at least 6 months validity remaining.
⛰️

Altitude Sickness (AMS) Protocol

Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) can affect anyone above 2,500m regardless of age or physical fitness. Our itineraries feature built-in acclimatization days.

  • 💧 Hydration: Drink 3 to 4 liters of clean water daily.
  • 🚶‍♂️ Pace: Trek "Bistari, Bistari" (Slowly, slowly).
  • 🩺 Monitoring: Daily pulse oximeter blood oxygen monitoring by your guide.
  • 🚨 Golden Rule: Never ascend with symptoms; descend immediately if AMS persists.
🛡️

Travel & Emergency Heli Insurance

Travel insurance is mandatory for all high-altitude treks in Nepal. Your policy must explicitly cover:

  • 🚁 Emergency Helicopter Evacuation up to 6,000 meters altitude.
  • 🏥 High-altitude medical treatment & hospital admission coverage.
  • ✈️ Flight cancellations and lost baggage protection.
  • Recommended Providers: World Nomads, Global Rescue, Allianz.
🎒

Essential Gear Checklist

Trekking gear can be purchased or rented in Kathmandu (Thamel). Core items include:

  • 🧥 Thermal base layers & Down Jacket (-15°C rating).
  • 🥾 Broken-in waterproof trekking boots & merino wool socks.
  • 🛌 Sleeping bag (-15°C) & lightweight trekking poles.
  • 🔋 Power bank, UV sunglasses, sunblock, & water purification tablets.
