- **Government of Nepal**: Registered Company under the Department of Industry.
- **Kathmandu Metropolitan City**: Licensed Tourism Business.
- **Inland Revenue Department**: Registered for PAN/VAT tax compliance.
- **TAAN (Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal)**: Active registered corporate member.
- **NMA (Nepal Mountaineering Association)**: Registered member for peak climbing and expedition management.
- **Nepal Tourism Board (NTB)**: Certified and authorized national adventure operator.

### Guide & Staff Training Credentials:
1. **Professional Trekking Guide Training (NATHM)**: All lead guides are trained, certified, and officially licensed by the Nepal Academy of Tourism and Hotel Management.
2. **First Aid & Wilderness Emergency Response (NATHM)**: Certified field staff trained in mountain triage, Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) recognition, and remote wilderness emergency protocols.
3. **Responsible Tourism & Eco-Trekking (TAAN)**: Environmental awareness, Leave No Trace (LNT) principles, and fair treatment / ethical porter welfare standards.
4. **Mountain Safety & High-Altitude Physiology (NATHM)**: Acclimatization pacing, pulse oximeter monitoring, and crisis risk management.
5. **Mountain Rescue Training (NMA)**: Professional emergency evacuation coordination and helicopter rescue protocols.
6. **Mountaineering & Peak-Climbing Training (NMA)**: Certified climbing Sherpas and guides equipped for technical peaks (Mera Peak, Island Peak, Lobuche Peak, Pisang Peak, Ama Dablam).
7. **Nepalese Culture & Heritage Destination Knowledge**: Deep historical and ethnographic knowledge across Gurung, Sherpa, Tamang, and Tibetan Buddhist communities.
