Rules & Regulations for Restricted Area Trekking in Nepal

To protect fragile Himalayan cultures, natural ecosystems, and border security, the Nepal Department of Immigration classifies specific regions as **Restricted Areas**. Trekkers visiting these zones must adhere to the following mandatory conditions:

  1. Mandatory Registered Guide: Independent or solo trekking without an officially licensed guide is strictly illegal.
  2. Minimum Group Size of Two: At least two non-Nepali trekkers are required for RAP permit issuance.
  3. Processing Through Local Agency: Permits must be issued via a registered Nepalese trekking company like Namaste Hiking Trek.
