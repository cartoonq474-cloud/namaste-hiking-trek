Himalayan Geography

Nepal's Premier Trekking Regions

From the towering peaks of Everest to the forbidden kingdom of Upper Mustang, discover the distinct mountain geography and cultures of Nepal.

Khumbu Himalayan Range

Everest (Khumbu) Region

The world-famous home of Mount Everest (8,848.86m), Lhotse (8,516m), and Ama Dablam (6,812m). Known for iconic Sherpa hospitality, ancient Tibetan Buddhist monasteries, and high alpine suspension bridges.

⛰️ Highest Point: 5,545 m 📜 Permits: Sagarmatha NP + Khumbu Permit ☀️ Best Season: Mar-May, Sep-Nov

Annapurna Conservation Area

Annapurna Massif Region

Offers incredible landscape diversity—from subtropical banana groves and dense rhododendron forests to the arid rain-shadow terrain of Manang and Mustang. Features famous circuits and short sunrise hikes.

⛰️ Highest Pass: Thorong La (5,416 m) 📜 Permits: ACAP + TIMS Card ☀️ Best Season: Mar-May, Oct-Dec
Explore Annapurna Region Hub →
Langtang National Park

Langtang Valley Region

Known as the "Valley of Glaciers", Langtang is closest to Kathmandu. Home to the Tamang ethnic group, Yak cheese factories, and sacred Gosainkunda alpine lakes.

⛰️ Highest Viewpoint: Tserko Ri (4,984 m) 📜 Permits: Langtang NP + TIMS ☀️ Best Season: Mar-May, Sep-Nov
Explore Langtang Region Hub →
Restricted Trekking Zone

Manaslu Circuit Region

A pristine restricted area circumnavigating Mount Manaslu (8,163m), the 8th highest mountain in the world. Authentic Tibetan villages, Larkya La Pass, and quiet wilderness.

⛰️ Highest Pass: Larkya La (5,106 m) 📜 Permits: Restricted Permit + MCAP + ACAP ☀️ Best Season: Mar-May, Sep-Nov
Explore Manaslu Region Hub →
