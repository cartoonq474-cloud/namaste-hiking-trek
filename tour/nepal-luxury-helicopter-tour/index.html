Day-by-Day Itinerary

DAY 1

VIP Airport Arrival in Kathmandu

Private luxury Mercedes/SUV transfer to 5-Star Hotel (Dwarika’s or Marriott). Fine dining briefing.

DAY 2

Everest Base Camp & Kala Patthar Helicopter Excursion

Fly by private helicopter over Lukla, Syangboche, Kala Patthar (5,545m), and Khumbu glacier. Champagne breakfast at Hotel Everest View.

DAY 3

Helicopter Transfer to Pokhara & Annapurna Aerial Flight

Helicopter transfer past Langtang to Pokhara. Aerial flight over Annapurna Base Camp & Machhapuchhre peak.

DAY 4

Pokhara Luxury Leisure & Lakeside Spa Day

Private boat ride on Phewa Lake, body massage treatment at luxury spa resort, and lakeside fine dining.

DAY 5

Fly back to Kathmandu & VIP Departure

Private return flight to Kathmandu and VIP airport transfer.
