Day by Day Schedule

Detailed Tour Itinerary

DAY 1

Drive to Gorkha & Explore Gorkha Durbar

Drive from Kathmandu to historic Gorkha hill. Tour Gorkha Durbar palace and Kalika temple.

DAY 2

Drive to Bandipur Hill Station

Continue drive to hilltop Newari town of Bandipur. Sunset walk along the cobblestone bazaar.

DAY 3

Bandipur Village Walk & Siddha Cave Excursion

Hike to Siddha Cave (largest cave in Nepal) and visit local silkworm farms and Gurung villages.

DAY 4

Return Drive to Kathmandu

Enjoy final mountain views over Marsyangdi valley before drive back to Kathmandu.
