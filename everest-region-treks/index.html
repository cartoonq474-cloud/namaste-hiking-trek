Complete Everest Region Trekking Guide

The Khumbu region in northeastern Nepal is widely celebrated as the world's premiere mountain destination. Home to Mount Everest (8,848.86m), Lhotse (8,516m), Makalu (8,485m), and Cho Oyu (8,188m), the region combines awe-inspiring high-altitude scenery with the deep Tibetan Buddhist culture of the indigenous Sherpa people.

Key Landmarks of the Khumbu

  • Lukla Airport (2,860m): The thrilling gateway airfield to Sagarmatha National Park.
  • Namche Bazaar (3,440m): The bustling Sherpa capital with mountain bakeries, gear shops, and panoramic views of Ama Dablam.
  • Tengboche Monastery (3,860m): The spiritual heart of Khumbu, offering morning chants against the backdrop of Mount Everest.
  • Kala Patthar (5,545m): The famous viewpoint summit delivering face-to-face vistas of Everest's south face and Nuptse.

Everest Trekking Permits & Regulations

All trekkers visiting the Everest region require two official permits, obtainable at Lukla or Monjo checkpoint:

1. Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality Permit: NPR 3,000 (~$23 USD) 2. Sagarmatha National Park Entry Permit: NPR 3,000 (~$23 USD)

Frequently Asked Questions

How fit do I need to be for Everest Base Camp?

Good cardiovascular fitness and stamina are essential. You don't need technical climbing experience, but training with stair climbing and weighted backpack walks 2-3 months prior is strongly recommended.

What are the teahouse accommodations like?

Teahouses offer comfortable twin-share rooms with mattresses, blankets, and communal dining rooms heated by yak-dung stoves. Wi-Fi and hot showers are available for a small fee.
