Clinical High-Altitude Protocols

The "Namaste 360°" Daily Safety System

We don't hope for good weather and good health; we actively manage every medical parameter. Here is how our clinical safety routine protects you each day on the trail.

07:00 AM • Morning

Vitals & SpO2 Check

Your lead guide uses a calibrated digital pulse oximeter to check your blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) and resting pulse rate. Readings are logged in your personal health log to monitor overnight acclimatization.

During Trek • Pacing

Controlled Ascent Pacing

We enforce a steady, unhurried "Bistari, Bistari" (slowly, slowly) pace, capping sleeping elevation gains to safe physiological limits and scheduling mandatory acclimatization days in Namche Bazaar and Dingboche.

Midday • Nutrition

Kitchen & Water Audit

Guides inspect teahouse kitchens for food hygiene, ensuring all meals are cooked fresh to order. Purified or Katadyn micro-filtered drinking water is provided to guarantee 3–4 liters of daily hydration.

07:00 PM • Evening

Lake Louise Scorecard

A second oximetry check is performed before bed, accompanied by a quick Lake Louise AMS symptom evaluation (headache, nausea, fatigue). Kathmandu base is updated nightly via satellite or cellular contact.

Zero-Delay Emergency Airborne Rescue Protocol
In the unlikely event of severe altitude illness or injury, our Kathmandu headquarters maintains direct dispatch lines with Nepal's premier helicopter operators. Evacuations are coordinated immediately without waiting for insurance paperwork delays.
Human Dignity in the High Mountains

Our Ethical Porter Welfare Charter: 20–22 kg Strict Cap

Porters carry the heartbeat of Himalayan expeditions, yet many travel companies treat them as cheap muscle. We firmly reject the common 30 kg industry practice as unsafe and unfair.

✓
20–22 kg Strict Load Limit: Split evenly between two trekkers (10–11 kg per client bag). We weigh duffel bags before departing Kathmandu.
✓
Full 4-Season Alpine Clothing & Footwear: Heavy thermal jackets, wind pants, warm fleece, high-traction mountain boots, woolen gloves, and UV alpine sunglasses.
✓
Comprehensive Emergency & Medical Insurance: 100% full coverage for accidental medical treatments and emergency high-altitude helicopter rescue, identical to our lead guides.
✓
The Porter-to-Guide Apprenticeship Program: We sponsor English lessons, wilderness first aid training, and NATHM guide licensing so promising porters can advance into fully licensed trekking guides (e.g., our senior guide Jiban Gurung).

Government-Authorized Industry Certifications & Bonded Security

Under Nepalese law, mountain expeditions, national park permits, and foreign currency transactions require strict statutory registration. Namaste Hiking Trek holds full authorization and permanent financial bonding with all national bodies.

Tourism License

Department of Tourism (DoT)

Licensed adventure and trekking agency under the Ministry of Culture, Tourism & Civil Aviation. Authorizes operations across all Nepalese mountain trails.

National Board

Nepal Tourism Board (NTB)

Certified national tourism operator complying with safety frameworks, environmental standards, and TIMS card management.

Corporate Member

Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal (TAAN)

Active corporate member maintaining ethical porter standards, trail conservation, and standardized guide wages across all routes.

Mountaineering Body

Nepal Mountaineering Association (NMA)

Authorized peak climbing operator managing expedition permits, alpine gear standards, and high-altitude rescue logistics for technical peaks.

FX Authorization

Nepal Rastra Bank (Central Bank)

Fully authorized to accept, process, and secure foreign currency transactions (USD, EUR, AUD, GBP) under state banking protection.

Eco Stewardship

Kathmandu Environmental Education (KEEP)

Aligned with Leave No Trace (LNT) codes, environmental awareness, and porter clothing welfare standards across high mountain passes.

100% Bonded Financial Protection for International Bookings

Namaste Hiking Trek maintains statutory cash security deposits and bank guarantees lodged with the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank. Your advance funds are legally bonded and protected from insolvency or trip cancellation risks.
