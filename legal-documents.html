Why Legal Registration Protects Your Trek

In remote Himalayan terrain, legal licensing is not just a formality—it is the bedrock of your financial security, medical evacuation safety, and genuine permit validity.

Central Bank Protected Payments

Your advance reservation deposit and wire transfers are received into state-audited corporate accounts regulated by Nepal Rastra Bank. Your financial investment is 100% bonded and protected against unauthorized commercial practices.

Guaranteed Helicopter Evacuation

During high-altitude medical emergencies or Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS), only government-licensed agencies possess standing authority to coordinate immediate helicopter extraction with the Civil Aviation Authority and international insurance underwriters.

Legitimate Government Permits

Government conservation permits (Sagarmatha, Annapurna, Langtang) and government liaison permits for restricted areas (Manaslu, Upper Mustang, Nar-Phu) are officially issued in your legal name, preventing trail entry denial or border fines.

Mandatory Crew Life & Medical Insurance

Statutory regulations mandate that every guide, porter, and kitchen crew member carrying your expedition equipment is fully insured against high-altitude accidents, frostbite, and illness, while adhering to strict 20–25 kg load limits.

Frequently Asked Questions About Legal Credentials

Direct answers to common questions about statutory licensing, government permits, and deposit security.

You can independently verify our operational licenses by contacting the Department of Tourism (DoT) in Kathmandu or browsing the verified member directory of the Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal (TAAN). Additionally, our corporate standing is public record at the Office of the Company Registrar, Ministry of Industry, Commerce and Supplies.

Under Nepalese immigration and tourism law, restricted regions including Manaslu Circuit, Upper Mustang, Nar-Phu, Tsum Valley, and Dolpo strictly require special Restricted Area Permits (RAP). These permits cannot be issued to independent travelers; they must be processed through an officially registered Nepalese trekking agency accompanied by a licensed government trekking guide and a minimum group of two travelers.

Our foreign currency bank accounts operate under the strict supervision of Nepal Rastra Bank (Central Bank). All payments receive formal banking receipts, and advance funds are held in compliant commercial corporate accounts. If trip disruptions occur due to weather or geopolitical events, our transparent deposit-transfer and credit policies are backed by legal agreements.
