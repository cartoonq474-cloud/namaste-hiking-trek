Namaste Hiking Trek Outreach Fund · 2016–2026

10 Years of Grassroots Mountain Action:
Where Tourism Revenue Directly Heals & Empowers

Nepal's trails are not merely trekking routes—they are our ancestral home. Since founding Namaste Hiking Trek in 2016 following the Gorkha earthquake, we have enforced one binding company charter: 10% of our annual net trekking profits are directly channeled into remote mountain school classrooms, emergency flood relief missions, and guaranteed living protections for our trail crews across Dhading, Ruby Valley, Rasuwa, and Nuwakot.

FEATURE DISPATCH · AUGUST 2026

Standing Together with Families Affected by the Rasuwa & Nuwakot Floods

In late August 2026, continuous cloudbursts along the Trishuli River basin triggered catastrophic flash floods and landslides, sweeping away riverside settlements and severing the main roadway into Rasuwa and upper Nuwakot. Over 100 families lost their homes, food stores, and livestock overnight.

Instead of waiting for bureaucratic relief channels, Namaste Hiking Trek immediately mobilized two 4WD mountain supply trucks loaded with 2.4 tons of emergency food cartons, insulated mattresses, heavy winter blankets, and cooking stoves. Our guides and local porters hand-carried supplies across broken bridge sections to reach cut-off families in Ward No. 3 and 4.

Itemized Aid Disbursed (Verified Receipt Record):
  • ✓ 100+ Displaced Families: Provided with complete dry ration crates (30kg rice, 5kg lentils, cooking oil, iodized salt) & hygienic cooking sets.
  • ✓ NPR 10 Lakh (NPR 1,000,000): In heavy-duty foam mattresses, thermal blankets, and clean water filtration units.
  • ✓ NPR 1,50,000 Cash Grants: Direct financial emergency support (NPR 50,000 each) given in person to 3 families who lost their primary breadwinner in the disaster.

VERIFIABLE COMMUNITY RECORD

The 10-Year Field Journal: 2016–2025

A transparent, year-by-year record of specific mountain projects, school winterization drives, and emergency medical missions.

Year 2025 Shree Kanyadevi Secondary School · Chalish Village, Dhading

"Warmth for Learning": Protecting 150+ Mountain Students from Freezing Winter Classrooms

At an elevation of 2,150 meters in the Ganesh Himal foothills, stone classrooms have no insulation or heating. During December and January, morning temperatures frequently drop to near freezing, causing severe winter absenteeism. Namaste Hiking Trek equipped every enrolled primary student with custom-tailored heavy fleece tracksuits, insulated jackets, woolen socks, and curriculum textbooks.

Verified Field Impact & Local Procurement:
  • ✓ 150+ Winter Tracksuits & Jackets: Tailored locally by seamstresses in Dhading to support the village economy.
  • ✓ 600+ Lined Notebooks & Pens: Delivered directly to classroom teachers to ensure no student was held back by lack of supplies.
  • ✓ Direct Teacher Testimony: Winter attendance increased by 42% compared to previous seasons.
Students of Shree Kanyadevi School in Chalish, Dhading proudly displaying their new winter uniforms and curriculum books.
Year 2024 Chitwan & Central Himalayan Foothills

Himalayan Healthcare Subsidies & Indigenous Cultural House Handover

When chronic illness strikes an elderly mountain villager living miles from the nearest road, ambulance transport to Kathmandu or Bharatpur often exceeds an entire family's annual income. In 2024, our Outreach Fund covered emergency hospital transfers and diagnostics for 12 elderly rural patients, while co-funding the construction of a traditional community gathering hall in Chitwan to preserve indigenous Gurung and Tamang customs.

Verified Field Impact:
  • ✓ 12 Critical Patient Subsidies: Full coverage of medical travel and specialist fees.
  • ✓ Community Cultural Hall: Co-financed stone masonry and roofing for a village community house dedicated to cultural folklore preservation and youth festivals.
Year 2021 Solukhumbu, Langtang & Dhading

Mountain Crew Lifeline: Sustaining 45+ Porters & Guides During the Global Shutdown

When international borders closed and commercial trekking ceased overnight during the pandemic, thousands of daily-wage mountain porters faced extreme economic hardship. Namaste Hiking Trek launched an internal solidarity fund, ensuring our roster of freelance guides, kitchen staff, and porters received continuous basic sustenance.

Verified Relief Delivered:
  • ✓ 45+ Trekking Crew Families: Disbursed monthly staple grocery packages (rice, lentils, cooking oil, salt) throughout the hardest months of the shutdown.
  • ✓ Zero Abandoned Staff: Every full-time and regular freelance crew member received living sustenance until international trails reopened.
Year 2019 Chalish & Tipling Villages · Ruby Valley

Clean Mountain Cooking: Slashing Toxic Indoor Smoke by 90%

Traditional open indoor hearths produce dense, toxic smoke that leads to severe chronic respiratory illness, especially among rural women and young children. In 2019, we introduced high-efficiency, smokeless biomass home stoves into isolated Ruby Valley households.

Verified Environmental & Health Metrics:
  • ✓ 90% Smoke Emission Cut: Clean combustion technology substantially reduced indoor particulate matter and carbon monoxide exposure.
  • ✓ Forest Conservation: Reduced domestic firewood consumption by over 50%, lessening pressure on fragile sub-alpine rhododendron and pine forests.
Year 2017 Chalish & Khading Villages · Ganesh Himal

Bridging the Mountain Divide: Solar-Powered Digital Classrooms

Remote mountain students deserve the same digital learning opportunities as city children. In 2017, partnering with rural education initiatives, Namaste Hiking Trek equipped two off-grid schools with rugged, low-power laptops pre-loaded with offline digital curriculums, multimedia math programs, and English dictionaries.

Verified Hardware & Syllabus Installed:
  • ✓ 16 Pre-Loaded Digital Laptops: Delivered and configured across Chalish (11 units) and Khading (5 units) primary schools.
  • ✓ Solar Battery Banks: Installed photovoltaic panels and heavy-duty battery storage to provide continuous daytime power in off-grid classrooms.
Students exploring digital educational software for the first time in their mountain classroom.

DIGNITY & HUMAN RIGHTS ON THE TRAIL

Our Ethical Porter & Trail Dignity Standard

No trekker should reach a mountain pass at the cost of an overloaded or exploited porter. We hold ourselves to strict, measurable working standards in full compliance with TAAN and IPPG guidelines.

DIGITAL SCALE VERIFICATION

The Strict 22 kg Maximum Load Standard

We assign one porter for every two trekkers. Each client duffel bag is strictly limited to 10–12 kg. Together with the porter's own personal sleeping bag and warm layers (approx. 3 kg), the maximum combined load never exceeds 22–25 kg.

  • ✓ Weighed Before Departure: Checked on luggage scales in Kathmandu or trailhead airports.
  • ✓ No Exceptions: Any excess weight must be carried in an additional porter bag at client expense.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, factual answers on how our Outreach Fund is audited, how porters are protected, and how travelers can participate.

The fund is financed entirely by dedicating 10% of Namaste Hiking Trek's corporate net profit at the close of every fiscal year. We do not tack an extra "charity surcharge" onto your trek invoice. When you book a trip at our transparent published prices, you are automatically funding these relief operations, school supplies, and crew protections.
Every client duffel bag is weighed on digital luggage scales before departing Kathmandu or domestic airfields (Lukla/Pokhara). If a client's bag exceeds 12 kg, excess weight must be transferred or a second porter hired. Furthermore, our lead guides carry personal responsibility: if a porter is found overloaded on trail, the guide faces internal operational sanctions.
Yes. If you are trekking in the Ganesh Himal / Ruby Valley or Langtang region, our itineraries can be adjusted to include a stop at Chalish Village. Travelers can bring compact educational materials (storybooks, stationery, colored pens, warm gloves). Founder Lal Gurung coordinates these handovers directly with headmaster Ram Bahadur Tamang so items reach the children with complete transparency.
Porters receive the exact same medical protocols as our paying international clients. Every crew member is insured with Himalayan Rescue Association (HRA) recognized mountain insurance. If a porter suffers from severe altitude sickness, they are accompanied down to a lower altitude immediately by an assistant guide, and if necessary, an emergency rescue helicopter is dispatched without hesitation.
