Why Trek in Langtang?

  • ✓ Accessible starting point just a 6-hour drive from Kathmandu
  • ✓ Breathtaking views of Langtang Lirung (7,234m) and Dorje Lakpa
  • ✓ Unique Tamang Buddhist heritage and authentic village homestays
  • ✓ Sacred high-altitude Gosaikunda alpine lakes
  • ✓ Traditional yak cheese factory at Kanjin Gompa
  • ✓ Quiet, scenic alternative to crowded EBC and Annapurna trails
