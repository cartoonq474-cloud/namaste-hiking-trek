Our Field Support Crew & Porters

Porters and assistant crew are the true backbone of Himalayan expeditions. We enforce non-negotiable ethical standards for every member of our trail family.

⚖️

20–25 kg Load Cap

Strict load limitations checked and weighed before leaving trailheads. Never overloaded.

🛡️

Medical & Evacuation Insurance

Every porter is insured for medical treatment and emergency helicopter evacuation up to 6,000m.

🧥

Alpine Gear & Lodging

Windproof jackets, insulated boots, warm sleeping gear, and paid teahouse beds and meals.

💵

Fair & Direct Wages

Wages paid above the TAAN minimum directly to porters without middleman deductions.
