About & Mountain Journey

Santa Bahadur Gurung brings a decade of dedicated guiding experience rooted in Dhading. Known for his disciplined health-monitoring protocols, Santa takes twice-daily SpO2 and pulse readings for all his trekkers, ensuring early identification of any altitude issues.

He possesses an intimate knowledge of local Himalayan flora, fauna, and geological formations, turning everyday trail walks into enriching educational experiences.

Santa is widely praised for his meticulous teahouse coordination and for ensuring that every porter on his team is well-equipped, rested, and treated with complete dignity.
