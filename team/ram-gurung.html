About & Mountain Journey

Hailing from Dhading, Ram Gurung has spent 6+ years leading treks through the central and eastern Himalayas. Known for his quiet confidence, reliable weather instincts, and caring trail demeanour, Ram ensures that groups never overexert themselves on steep acclimatization days.

He takes personal responsibility for checking every trekker's hydration and boots each morning, preventing blisters and altitude fatigue before they can develop.

Ram's warm, gentle guidance makes him especially popular with family groups and travelers looking for a supportive, peaceful mountain experience.
