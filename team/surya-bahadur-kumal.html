About & Mountain Journey

Surya Bahadur Kumal has guided Himalayan trails for over 8 years. Highly fluent in Korean as well as English, Surya is a key asset for international trekking groups from East Asia and Western nations alike.

He specializes in high-altitude trail coordination, managing porter rotations with care, and introducing guests to local Tibetan-Buddhist cultural customs.

His guests regularly highlight his enthusiasm, reliable health monitoring, and proactive coordination with lodge kitchens for dietary accommodations.
