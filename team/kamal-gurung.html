About & Mountain Journey

Kamal Gurung brings 12+ years of mountain guiding experience combined with senior digital operations and guest communications. Growing up in Dhading, Kamal mastered the major trail networks of Everest, Annapurna, and Mustang before stepping into executive route consultation.

Specializing in custom itinerary design, Kamal excels at balancing trekking schedules with proper acclimatization days, helping first-time Himalayan hikers and seasoned mountaineers alike select the ideal season and route.

In addition to his administrative leadership, Kamal regularly returns to the trails to lead private expeditions, ensuring that our customer service always remains grounded in real mountain conditions.
