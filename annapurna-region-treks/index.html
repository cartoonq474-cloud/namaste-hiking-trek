Guide to Trekking in the Annapurna Region

The Annapurna Conservation Area (ACAP) in central Nepal is one of the world's premier hiking destinations. Spanning subtropical lowlands to high desert plateaus behind Thorong La Pass, Annapurna offers an unparalleled blend of ecological diversity and mountain splendor.

Top Attractions

  • Poon Hill (3,210m): Iconic viewpoint overlooking Dhaulagiri (8,167m) and Annapurna I.
  • Annapurna Sanctuary (4,130m): A sacred high-altitude basin ringed by 7,000m+ Himalayan giants.
  • Thorong La Pass (5,416m): The world's widest navigable mountain pass connecting Manang and Muktinath.
