## 6. Standard Inclusions & Guarantees

All standard trekking and climbing packages operated by Namaste Hiking Trek include:

- **Airport Transfers**: Private vehicle arrival and departure pick-up/drop at Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM).
- **Kathmandu Hotel Lodging**: Standard tourist-standard or 3-star boutique hotel in Thamel with complimentary breakfast.
- **Domestic Flights / Overland Transport**: Lukla / Pokhara flights (including domestic airport taxes) or private Scorpio 4WD jeep transport.
- **Teahouse Lodging & Full Board**: Twin-share mountain lodge rooms with 3 fresh daily meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner with tea/coffee).
- **Licensed Himalayan Guide**: Government-licensed, English-fluent mountain guide equipped with medical first-aid kit and oximeter.
- **Porter Support**: Strong local porters (1 porter for 2 trekkers, strictly following 20 kg weight restrictions).
- **All Permits & Fees**: TIMS card, National Park conservation fees (Sagarmatha, Annapurna, Manaslu), and local government taxes.
- **Staff Insurance**: Comprehensive emergency medical and rescue insurance for all guides, cooks, and porters.
- **Farewell Dinner**: Authentic Nepali cultural dinner and celebration in Kathmandu upon completion.
