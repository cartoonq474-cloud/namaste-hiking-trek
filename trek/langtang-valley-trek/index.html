Langtang Region • Valley of Glaciers

Langtang Valley Trek

⏱️ Duration: 9 Days / 8 Nights ⛰️ Max Altitude: 4,773 m (Kyanjin Ri Peak) 🥾 Difficulty: Moderate ☀️ Best Season: Mar-May & Sep-Nov

Trek Overview

The 9-day Langtang Valley Trek is the closest alpine trekking region to Kathmandu. It offers a spectacular valley of glaciers, traditional Tamang heritage, yak pastures, and high peaks without the crowds of Everest or Annapurna.

Reach Kyanjin Gompa (3,870m) and climb Kyanjin Ri or Tserko Ri (4,773m) for outstanding vistas of Langtang Lirung (7,227m) and the border range.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 01: Arrival in Kathmandu & Trek Briefing

Welcome to Tribhuvan International Airport. Transfer to hotel in Thamel, gear check, and team introduction. (Altitude: 1,400m)

Day 02: Scenic Flight to Lukla (2,860m) & Trek to Phakding (2,610m)

35-minute mountain flight to Lukla's Tenzing-Hillary Airport. 3-4 hours gentle downhill hike through Sherpa hamlets to Phakding.

Day 03: Phakding to Namche Bazaar (3,440m)

Cross 5 iconic suspension bridges including Hillary Suspension Bridge. Final steep uphill climb into the vibrant Sherpa hub of Namche.

Day 04: Acclimatization Day in Namche (Hike to Everest View Hotel - 3,880m)

Essential acclimatization day. Hike to Syangboche and Everest View Hotel for first panorama of Everest, Lhotse, and Ama Dablam.

Day 05: Namche Bazaar to Tengboche (3,867m)

Descend to Imja Khola river before climbing through rhododendron forests to Tengboche Monastery, the spiritual center of Khumbu.

Day 06-08: Dingboche to Lobuche & Everest Base Camp Summit (5,364m)

Trek past Himalayan memorials at Thokla Pass to Gorak Shep. Reach Everest Base Camp on Day 8 and witness the Khumbu Icefall.

Day 09: Sunrise Summit of Kala Patthar (5,545m) & Descend to Pheriche

Early morning climb to Kala Patthar viewpoint for unhindered sunrise over Mt. Everest. Descend back towards Pheriche.

Day 10-14: Return Trek to Lukla & Flight to Kathmandu

Descend through Namche Bazaar back to Lukla for celebratory dinner. Morning flight back to Kathmandu and farewell celebration.
