Trek Overview

The 16-day Annapurna Circuit Trek is widely considered one of the ultimate classic long-distance trekking journeys in the world. Encircling the massive Annapurna Massif, this trek takes you through an astounding variety of landscapes — from lush subtropical pine forests and cascading waterfalls to barren Tibetan-style high plateaus and glacial valleys.

The emotional peak of the trek comes on Day 9 as you cross the high mountain pass of Thorong La (5,416 meters / 17,769 feet). Descending from the pass leads into the rain-shadow region of Mustang and the sacred pilgrimage town of Muktinath, revered by both Hindus and Buddhists.

You will also walk along the Kali Gandaki Gorge — the deepest gorge on Earth — framed by Annapurna I (8,091m) and Dhaulagiri (8,167m). Relax at the natural thermal hot springs in Tatopani before climbing to Poon Hill for a world-renowned sunrise over 14 Himalayan peaks.

16-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 01

Arrival in Kathmandu & Welcome Briefing

1,400m / 4,593ft ▲
Upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu, our representative welcomes you and transfers you to your hotel in Thamel. In the evening, attend a trip briefing with your lead Sherpa guide and enjoy a welcome Nepali dinner.
Day 02

Drive from Kathmandu to Besisahar & Dharapani

1,860m / 6,102ft ▼
Scenic drive along Trishuli and Marsyangdi river valleys past terrace farms to Besisahar. Switch to 4WD jeep to reach Dharapani village, the official entry point of the Annapurna Conservation Area. (7–8 hrs drive)
Day 03

Dharapani to Chame

2,670m / 8,760ft ▼
Trek through pine forests and along the Marsyangdi River. Pass traditional Gurung villages and enjoy breathtaking views of Annapurna II and Lamjung Himal as you arrive in Chame, district headquarters of Manang. (5–6 hrs hike)
Day 04

Chame to Pisang

3,200m / 10,498ft ▼
Walk through dense apple orchards and cross a suspension bridge. Gaze up at the sheer rock face of Paungda Danda rising 1,500m above the river before entering Upper Pisang with Tibetan-style prayer walls. (5–6 hrs hike)
Day 05

Pisang to Manang Village

3,540m / 11,614ft ▼
Take the upper route via Ghyaru and Ngawal for magnificent views of Annapurna III and Tilicho Peak. Descend into the dry, arid valley of Manang where Buddhist chortens line the stone pathways. (6–7 hrs hike)
Day 06

Acclimatization Day in Manang (Ice Lake / Gangapurna Hike)

3,540m / 11,614ft ▼
Critical acclimatization day. Take an active day-hike to Chongkor viewpoint or high altitude Ice Lake (Kicho Tal at 4,600m). In the afternoon, attend the Himalayan Rescue Association (HRA) lecture on altitude sickness.
Day 07

Manang to Yak Kharka

4,050m / 13,287ft ▼
Ascend gradually through alpine pastures above the tree line. Spot grazing yaks, blue sheep (bharal), and Himalayan eagles as you reach Yak Kharka lodge settlement. (4–5 hrs hike)
Day 08

Yak Kharka to Thorong Phedi / High Camp

4,450m / 14,599ft ▼
Walk along rocky scree slopes before crossing the Thorong Khola stream. Ascend steep switchbacks to reach Thorong Phedi base camp. Rest early for the pre-dawn pass crossing. (4–5 hrs hike)
Day 09

Thorong Phedi to Muktinath via Thorong La Pass (5,416m)

5,416m Max Altitude ▼
Start at 3:30 AM to climb Thorong La Pass (5,416m), marked by fluttering chortens and prayer flags. Celebrate the pinnacle triumph before descending 1,600m into Mustang to the sacred temples of Muktinath. (8–9 hrs hike)
Day 10

Muktinath to Jomsom via Kagbeni

2,720m / 8,923ft ▼
Visit Muktinath's 108 water spouts before descending through the windy river valley to Kagbeni, gateway to Upper Mustang. Continue along the Kali Gandaki riverbed to Jomsom town. (5–6 hrs hike)
Day 11

Jomsom to Tatopani Hot Springs

1,190m / 3,904ft ▼
Scenic drive or hike down through Marpha apple villages and Rupse Waterfall into Tatopani. Spend the afternoon relaxing in natural riverside thermal hot springs. (4–5 hrs drive)
Day 12

Tatopani to Ghorepani

2,860m / 9,383ft ▼
Ascend through terraced farmland and dense rhododendron forests to the ridge settlement of Ghorepani, offering breathtaking views of Dhaulagiri and Annapurna South. (6–7 hrs hike)
Day 13

Sunrise Hike to Poon Hill (3,210m) & Trek to Tadapani

3,210m / 10,531ft ▼
Early 4:30 AM hike up Poon Hill for sunrise over Dhaulagiri, Annapurna I, Machhapuchhre (Fishtail), and Nilgiri. Return to lodge for breakfast and trek to Tadapani. (6–7 hrs hike)
Day 14

Tadapani to Nayapul & Drive to Pokhara

822m / 2,696ft ▼
Descend through bamboo forests to Ghandruk village and Nayapul trailhead. Private vehicle drive to lakeside Pokhara hotel. Enjoy a hot shower and lakeside dinner celebration. (5 hrs hike + 2 hrs drive)
Day 15

Scenic Drive / Flight Pokhara to Kathmandu

1,400m / 4,593ft ▼
Return drive or optional 25-minute flight from Pokhara to Kathmandu. Afternoon free for souvenir shopping in Thamel. Evening farewell dinner with cultural performance.
Day 16

Final Airport Transfer & Departure

Departure ▼
Transfer to Kathmandu International Airport 3 hours prior to your scheduled flight home. Namaste & safe travels home!

Frequently Asked Questions

What permits are required for the Annapurna Circuit Trek?

You need two permits: the Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP) and the TIMS (Trekker's Information Management System) card. Both permits are included in our standard package and issued by us prior to departure.

How difficult is crossing Thorong La Pass (5,416m)?

Thorong La is a high alpine pass that requires good physical endurance and proper acclimatization. We include an essential acclimatization day in Manang (3,540m) and climb gradually over several days to ensure safe crossing.
